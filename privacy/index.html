Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: I collect only what I need to reply to you and do the work.

Who I am

This website is run by Aslam Local Visibility, based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. In this policy, “I” and “me” mean Aslam Local Visibility. To reach me about your data, use the contact page or book a call.

What information I collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, city (optional) and the message you write.
  • Booking: if you book through Cal.com, the details you enter there (such as name, email and chosen time) are collected by Cal.com and shared with me.
  • Technical data: like most websites, the hosting provider may record your IP address, browser type, device and the pages you request, to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Client work: if you become a client, the materials and access you give me, such as your logo, brand colors, photos and profile access.

I do not ask for payment details on this website, and there are no user accounts.

How I use it

  • To reply to your message and arrange your free visibility audit.
  • To provide the services you request.
  • To keep the website secure and working.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not use it for advertising.

Services that handle data for this site

  • Static Forms receives and delivers the contact form messages to me.
  • Cal.com handles call bookings when you use the booking link.
  • Cloudinary serves the images on this site. Loading an image may share your IP address with them.
  • YouTube videos load only after you click play, using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode. Once you play a video, Google’s own privacy policy applies to that viewing.
  • Hosting provider delivers the website files and may keep server logs.

Each of these has its own privacy policy, and I recommend reading theirs if you want details.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set its own cookies and does not currently use analytics or advertising trackers. Third-party services you choose to use, such as a YouTube video or the Cal.com booking page, may set their own cookies. If I add analytics in the future, I will update this policy.

How long I keep your information

I keep contact messages for as long as needed to respond to you and for ordinary business records. If you become a client, I keep project information while we work together and for a reasonable time afterward. You can ask me to delete your information at any time, unless I need to keep it for legal or accounting reasons.

Testimonials and case studies

I only publish a client’s name, testimonial or results with their permission, or as agreed in the Founding Client offer described in the Terms and Conditions.

Your choices and rights

You can ask me to tell you what information I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a particular purpose. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your local data protection law, and I will respect them where they apply. Contact me using the details above and I will respond within a reasonable time.

Where your data goes

I am based in Bangladesh, and the services listed above may process data in other countries. By using this site and sending me information, you understand your information may be transferred and processed outside your own country.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS, and I use reputable providers. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is for business owners and professionals. It is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy as the website or my services change. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
