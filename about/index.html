About Aslam

Who You’ll Work With

I’m Aslam. I help local coaches and consultants get found, and chosen, in their own city. I’m based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, and I work remotely with clients worldwide.

Good coaches and consultants often lose clients to people who simply show up better online. My job is to close that gap, using only organic methods: Reels, branded design, a strong Google Business Profile and genuine local engagement. I don’t run ads, and you will never pay for ads through me.

How We Work Together

  1. Free visibility audit

    We look at how you appear in local search today and decide which package fits.

  2. Monthly content session

    About one hour of your time each month for a content batch session. I handle the rest.

  3. I publish and build

    I create the content, keep your profiles current and stay active in your local community.

What I Need From You

  • Your logo and brand colors
  • A few photos
  • About 1 hour of your time per month

If you are comfortable on camera, Reels perform significantly better. If not, we can use your voice, your expertise and branded visuals.
